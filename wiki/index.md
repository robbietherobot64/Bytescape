# Bytescape Wiki

**Import your history, find your friends, own your presence.** Bytescape is an
open social platform for people leaving Facebook and Instagram — and for
Bluesky users who want richer profiles and feeds than the default client
offers. Built on AT Protocol, connecting you to Bluesky's network of 40+
million people, with ActivityPub integration (the fediverse) rolling out now.

Not designed to be addictive: utility over habit, a feed you control, a
profile that's actually yours.
