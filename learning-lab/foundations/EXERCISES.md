# Foundations Exercises

This pack gives you hands-on practice after the lessons.

## How to Use This Pack

1. Complete Lesson 00 to Lesson 05 first.
2. Do exercises in order.
3. Write your answers in a separate notes file or in progress-tracker.md.
4. Submit your answers in chat for grading.
5. Use answer-key.md only after attempting each exercise.

## Exercise Order

1. exercise-01-typescript-core.md
2. exercise-02-async-and-error-flow.md
3. exercise-03-rust-signatures-and-results.md
4. exercise-04-tauri-boundary-trace.md

## Scoring Rubric

- Each exercise has 10 points.
- Total: 40 points.

Readiness thresholds:

- 34 to 40: ready to move to Week 1 architecture lessons.
- 26 to 33: mostly ready, review weak sections first.
- 0 to 25: repeat foundations lessons and retry exercises.

## Rules

1. No guessing. Explain your reasoning.
2. If you are unsure, write your best hypothesis.
3. Keep answers short and precise.
